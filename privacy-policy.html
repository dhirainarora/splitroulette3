Privacy Policy

Remix Split Roulette

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Overview

Remix Split Roulette is a bill-splitting application that helps users distribute a bill amount among participants.

2. Information We Collect

Remix Split Roulette does not require users to create an account. The app may process information that users enter into the app, such as participant names and bill amounts, to provide its bill-splitting functionality.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the app.

3. Advertising

Remix Split Roulette uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information, including device or advertising identifiers and information related to advertising and app usage, in accordance with their applicable policies.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use apps that use its services, please refer to Google's applicable privacy information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including advertising providers such as Google AdMob, for purposes such as displaying and measuring advertisements.

5. Data Storage and Retention

We do not operate our own server for collecting or maintaining user accounts or personal profiles.

Information entered into the app for bill-splitting functionality is used to provide the requested functionality. Any information stored locally on the device remains subject to the device's local storage and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to the behavior of the Android operating system.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of user information. Third-party services used by the app may apply their own security and privacy practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Remix Split Roulette is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account creation or registration.

8. Your Choices

Users can choose what information they enter into the app. Users may also remove locally stored app data through Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.
